[Short Description] (Max 80 chars)

[Full Description] (Max 4000 chars)
Zorbit is a fast-paced 2D space arcade game where you launch your rocket from spinning planet orbits and land safely on the next planet with precise timing.

HOW TO PLAY
Your rocket orbits continuously around each planet. Tap the screen at the right moment to fire your rocket toward the next planet. Take your time to line up the perfect angle—just don't drift into deep space!

KEY FEATURES
• One-Tap Arcade Controls: Instant, responsive gameplay that is easy to learn and challenging to master.
• Dynamic Difficulty: Planet rotation and rocket speed scale up as your score climbs.
• Bonus Stars & Hazards: Grab +3 point stars while avoiding -3 rotating obstacles.
• Weekly Zorbits Leaderboard: Pick your unique player name, compete on the live weekly leaderboard, and check Past Weeks champions.
• Offline Play & Auto-Sync: Play anywhere offline—your scores sync automatically as soon as you're back online.
• Smooth WebGL Visuals: Crisp 60 FPS graphics, dynamic camera zoom, and particle bursts.

[Release Notes] (v2.0.5)
• Upgraded to WebGL rendering for smoother 60 FPS performance.
• Added live Weekly Zorbits leaderboard and Past Weeks archive.
• Added unique player usernames and automatic offline score sync.
